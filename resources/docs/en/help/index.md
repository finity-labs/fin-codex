Every page of this application can carry its own help. The question-mark button in the top bar opens a drawer on the right, and the number on the button is how many articles talk about the page you are on.

## Opening the drawer

:::steps
1. Click the question-mark button in the top bar, or press the keyboard shortcut shown at the bottom of the drawer.

2. The **This page** tab lists the articles written for the current screen. When there is only one, it opens straight away.

3. **Browse** shows every article, grouped by section, whatever page you are on.
:::

## Searching

Type into the search field at the top of the drawer. Results come from every article you are allowed to read, and the matching words are highlighted in each hit. Clear the field to return to where you were.

> [!TIP] Links inside an article
> A link to another help article opens it in the drawer. The **Back** arrow in the drawer's header takes you to the previous article.

## The help center

**Open the help center** at the bottom of the drawer opens the same articles as a full page, with the section tree beside them, for reading longer articles or sharing a link to one.
