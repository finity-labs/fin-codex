Fill in your name, your email address and a password, repeat the password, and press **Sign up**. You are signed in straight away.

## Your password

Choose a password you do not use anywhere else. A longer phrase beats a short, complicated one. It has to match in both fields, letter for letter.

## Verifying your email

Some applications ask you to confirm your email address before you can go on. A message with a link arrives at the address you gave; open the link and you are done. If it does not arrive, check the spam folder, then ask for a new one on the verification page.
