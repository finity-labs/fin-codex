A message with a verification link was sent to the address of your account. Open the link and the application lets you in.

## No message arrived

- Check the spam folder.
- Press **Resend notification** on this page to get a fresh message.
- If the address shown here is wrong, sign out, sign in again and correct it in your profile, or ask an administrator.

The link is personal and expires after a while; request a new one rather than reusing an old message.
