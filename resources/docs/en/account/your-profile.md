Your profile holds the name the application shows for you, the email address you sign in with, and your password.

## Changing your password

Enter the new password twice and save. You stay signed in on this device; other devices ask for the new password the next time.

## Changing your email address

Enter the new address and save. Sign in with the new address from then on. If the application verifies email addresses, a confirmation message goes to the new one.

> [!TIP] Locked fields
> A field you cannot edit is managed elsewhere, by an administrator or by the sign-in provider your account comes from.
